"7 Cures for a Lean Purse"

This chapter outlines practical rules for building personal wealth and financial stability. The core cures focus on starting your savings by keeping a portion of what you earn, controlling your expenditures, making your gold multiply through sensible investments, guarding your treasures from loss, making your dwelling a profitable investment, insuring a future income, and increasing your ability to earn

"5 Laws of Gold"

The second chapter details the fundamental nature of money and wealth through the story of Hadan. The five laws explain that gold comes easily to those who save a portion, works diligently for a wise owner, clings to the protection of cautious owners, slips away from unfamiliar investments, and flees from impossible earnings

Personal Reflection & Biggest Takeaway

Reading The Richest Man in Babylon by George S. Clason completely shifted how I view financial management, especially as a university student running my own tech retail business[cite: 2]. My biggest takeaway is the importance of paying yourself first and ensuring that capital works to generate compounding returns rather than being entirely consumed by short-term expenses.

I can align these principles with my life by systematically setting aside a fixed percentage of my business earnings and university project funds into savings and investments, treating financial discipline with the same rigor I apply to software engineering and problem-solving.
